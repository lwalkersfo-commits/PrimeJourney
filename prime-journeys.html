Teacher notes
  • Each column counts how many times that prime is in the number. Multiplying by 3 adds a counter to the 3 column; dividing by 3 removes one. You can only divide if there is a counter to remove, so the board shows why 56 ÷ 3 fails.
  • Start and target numbers are never prime. Numbers on the way can be anything, including 1 and primes (56 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1 → 3 → 9 → 27).
  • Fewest steps = counters to remove + counters to add. Ask pupils to predict it before they start.
  • Fade the scaffold: untick "Show target on board", then "Show prime factors", then hide the board and do it on mini-whiteboards.
  • Relay mode chains journeys like 56 → 27 → 63 → 48.
